## Relationship to the full framework update

These issues supplement the original 32 decisions plus C33–C52 in the [expanded evidence review](FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md), informed by seven evidence waves. The broader update still includes trust lifecycle, pre-trust execution, final-effect authorization, evidence independence, consumable-authority limits, organizational AIMS, all five pillars, AISM threat profiles and readiness, and reciprocal CSF evidence. Every candidate keeps its own why/why-not, trade-off, research note, parent-control depth, tests and migration disposition. Existing-control placement requires substantive requirements and a research rationale.

The review package must contain a source-to-decision ledger, individually drafted control requirements, revised research, profile applicability, AISM and schema compatibility, reference implementations, executed evidence, and synchronized navigation and machine data. Record what shipped and what failed. Proposed grading changes require historical comparison and schema/version decisions. Control counts follow approved canonical data.
