## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-08

Initial contract preview.

- Defines AI SAFE2 Core separately from the CLI and product workflows.
- Adds provider-neutral adapter and evidence contracts.
- Adds a game-development profile and dual Claude/Codex guidance.
- Places MCP Pro and Agency Check on the same evidence foundation while
  preserving their distinct professional and everyday decision experiences.
- Registers NEXUS, Sovereign Runtime, and Drift/Love Equation as adapters.
- Adds pinned-upstream and isolated-upgrade policy.
- Adds validation and decision-record generation scripts.
- Hardens terminal decision records so approvals and rejections require a
  named human, timestamp, and rationale.
- Adds deterministic, revision-bound evidence evaluation without granting
  merge or release authority.
- Makes the starter CI classify every pull request and retain its route as a
  replayable artifact.
- Defines canonical evidence-digest semantics and truthful failed/unavailable
  execution requirements.
