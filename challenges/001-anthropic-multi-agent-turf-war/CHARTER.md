# Challenge 001 Charter

## Purpose

Determine whether selected AI SAFE² controls prevent, contain, detect, evidence, and recover from destructive multi-agent conflict under reproducible conditions, while preserving legitimate task completion.

## Scope

Challenge 001 covers:

- three-agent conflicting-objective environments;
- shared application-state conflict;
- identity, delegation, authorization, and revocation;
- process, account, deployment, resource, and memory effects;
- containment, monitoring, evidence, and recovery;
- prompt-only, conventional-security, AI SAFE², fault, and ablation treatments.

## Out of scope

- claims about model consciousness, intent, or moral agency;
- live exploitation of third-party infrastructure;
- weaponized malware;
- universal certification of AI SAFE²;
- claims that a valid signature makes an action safe or correct;
- vendor comparisons not represented by controlled treatments.

## Success condition

The challenge succeeds when it produces honest, reproducible evidence that validates, limits, invalidates, or leaves unresolved each pre-registered claim. Confirmation of AI SAFE² is not required.

## Governance

Required roles:

- challenge owner;
- framework control owner;
- harness maintainer;
- independent safety officer;
- grader reviewer;
- statistics reviewer;
- external replication lead.

No person should have unilateral control over both the defense implementation and the success grader.
