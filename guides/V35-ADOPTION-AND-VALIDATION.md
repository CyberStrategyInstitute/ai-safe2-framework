## Keep versions separate

Framework v3.5 is a source candidate. Core Gateway remains component v3.0 with its original runtime evidence identity; importing a v3.5 catalog does not add all runtime requirements. NEXUS, scanner, CLI, SDKs, profiles and copied skills retain their own component/schema and publication boundaries. [Component versions](../docs/COMPONENT-VERSIONS.md) records those distinctions.

## Validate your path

1. Pin the reviewed checkout and identify model, harness, tools, state, policies, target and owner.
2. Resolve the control and applicable child clauses. Record justified exclusions and missing mappings.
3. Declare the intended effect and success criteria before execution or grading.
4. Observe the protected state, including negative cases, legitimate utility, partial effects and unknown outcomes.
5. Bind evidence to revision, scope, time and independent observer. Record limitations and recovery.
6. Review source applicability and evidence before changing a mapping, release claim or operating decision.

## Existing guides and historical material

Keep stack-specific hardening and troubleshooting guides for their stated versions. Use this guide as the current v3.5 adoption entry point. The [surface review](../docs/REPOSITORY-SURFACE-REVIEW-V35.md) identifies those dispositions. No guide is deleted merely because its original version differs.
