# UAS v2.0 candidate profile

This v3.5 companion retains 27 UAS requirement IDs and the three-layer structure. It changes the human and fairness acceptance interpretation, so adopters must explicitly migrate from v1.0. The v1 profile and its protocol remain baseline artifacts. The profile is CSI-authored and requires review of the actual applicable procurement instrument before a contractual claim.

Use [the v2 catalog](uas-profile-v2.json) as the machine-readable owner. H1 requires evidence of independent criteria, comprehension and retained capability using comparable tasks. H8 requires practical, justified intervention and decision authority. CTSS remains the CSF threat assessment. An override frequency alone establishes no healthy range or retained agency.

## Acceptance requirements

- Record task, model/system revision, population, protocol, accessibility arrangements, initial criteria where available, assisted result and relevant comparable unaided result.
- Measure justified acceptance and rejection, actual intervention, missing evidence, scope expansion and recovery. Include correct and incorrect recommendations and operational time constraints.
- For neutrality and fairness, declare the metric, sample and power rationale where applicable, effect sizes, uncertainty, relevant subgroups and conflicting objectives. A nonsignificant difference alone establishes no equivalence or fairness.
- Distinguish factual accuracy, political/viewpoint treatment, protected-group fairness and accessibility. CSI-defined sample sizes and numerical thresholds must be justified for the deployment instead of represented as externally required.
- Use independent reviewer conflict and competence records. Record data minimization, retention, participant consent and limitations. Retest after material changes in persona, memory, evidence routing and effective authority.

## Reciprocal CSF integration

Map independent criteria and evidence completeness to M-006c, M-007c and M-007c2; actual intervention and scope-specific approval to M-006g and M-006g2; source independence to M-S06g; memory, bystander consent and exit to M-005g. Pin the owning CSF revision at use. These are supporting relationships requiring a paired human/machine case, rather than an equivalence claim.

One case links machine authorization, delegated scope, effect and verified stopping to the human's criteria, evidence received, comprehension, justified reliance and ability to intervene. Keep separate results and privacy-controlled evidence references. Coordinate the reciprocal mapping in CSF; this PR changes the AI SAFE2 side.
