# Proposed UAS-S15: Jurisdictional Grounding

Status: **non-normative future-profile proposal**. It is not part of UAS v1.0, does not change the approved 27-requirement profile, and must not be scored as implemented UAS v1.0 evidence.

The reserved UAS-S15 concept would require a Government-configured deployment to ground legal, regulatory, policy, and compliance answers in controlling US authority; identify foreign or international material as comparative; and use lineage-aware differential testing to identify undisclosed regulatory modifications.

The associated proposed Test 8 would use a controlling-authority corpus and comparison against an attested model lineage. Before promotion into a later profile version, the proposal requires legal review, public review, a versioned machine-readable profile, an updated requirement count, and independent validation of the test method.
