# Idempotency and Side-Effect Ledger | C18

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5 candidate. The requirements below are proposed normative content on this branch. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Evidence waves 01-06; C18 disposition in docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md and docs/FRAMEWORK-UPDATE-ISSUES.md

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

Idempotency, conflict resolution, compensation and unknown outcomes reuse recovery obligations. APAY retains settlement-specific details.

The primary owner is **A2.7 Idempotency and Side-Effect Ledger**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-MAY-04 Grackle Cross-Task Authorization Bypass

**Reported facts in the supplied review** Several MCP tools omitted ancestry and workspace checks. The MCP server used a full server API key for backend calls, while the backend performed no caller-specific authorization. Scoped agents could perform cross-task and cross-session mutations.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E2.** Reviewed advisory with a traced authorization failure across components.

**Limits** No patched version was listed at publication, and real-world exploitation was not established.

[Source reference 1](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-f9ff-5x35-7gfw)

### OBS-2026-SEP-03 ActGov Policy-Constrained Action Validation

**Reported facts in the supplied review** ActGov converts tool actions into finite policy records and validates each action against task-scoped authority. Policy updates use SMT-based counterexample checking. Evaluations on AgentDojo and AgentDyn reported reduced indirect-prompt-injection success while retaining utility.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3-.** Formal policy model and multi-benchmark evaluation, but no independent deployment evidence.

**Limits** Tool specifications and benign traces may omit future behaviors. Semantic abstraction can lose security-relevant detail.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24446)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **A2.7.R01** Define transaction boundaries, shared-state ownership, locks, conflict handling, retries and compensation.
- **A2.7.R02** Preserve unknown outcomes until an authoritative state observation or accountable reconciliation resolves them.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Retries, partial effects and concurrent state. Measure duplicates, conflicts and observed recovery with a synthetic destination.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
