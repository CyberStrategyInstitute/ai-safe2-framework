# Final-Effect Authorization | C03

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5 candidate. The requirements below are proposed normative content on this branch. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Evidence waves 01-06; C03 disposition in docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md and docs/FRAMEWORK-UPDATE-ISSUES.md

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

The action can become inadmissible after approval even with correct identity and tool permissions. CP.4 remains the supporting control plane. A standalone requirement costs adapter and commit-boundary work.

The primary owner is **CP.12 Final-Effect Authorization**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-APR-01 Symbolic Guardrails for Domain-Specific Agents

**Reported facts in the supplied review** A review of 80 agent-security and safety benchmarks found that 85% did not state verifiable requirements. The authors found 74% of evaluated domain requirements could be enforced symbolically, with most requiring simple checks. Released artifacts support reproduction.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Broad benchmark review, enforceability analysis, empirical evaluation, and public artifacts.

**Limits** Symbolic rules work only where requirements can be represented precisely. They do not solve ambiguous intent, incomplete policy, or unknown future actions.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15579)

### OBS-2026-MAY-02 AIRGuard Runtime Authority Control

**Reported facts in the supplied review** AIRGuard defined authority confusion: untrusted information may influence reasoning but must not authorize effects. It normalizes tool calls, derives step-level authority, tracks source and target trust, simulates sensitive effects, and makes decisions before execution.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3-.** Implemented runtime design and empirical evaluation, but not independent operational validation.

**Limits** Authority derivation can fail when the original task is ambiguous or maliciously framed.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28914)

### OBS-2026-SEP-03 ActGov Policy-Constrained Action Validation

**Reported facts in the supplied review** ActGov converts tool actions into finite policy records and validates each action against task-scoped authority. Policy updates use SMT-based counterexample checking. Evaluations on AgentDojo and AgentDyn reported reduced indirect-prompt-injection success while retaining utility.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3-.** Formal policy model and multi-benchmark evaluation, but no independent deployment evidence.

**Limits** Tool specifications and benign traces may omit future behaviors. Semantic abstraction can lose security-relevant detail.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24446)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **CP.12.R01** Immediately before committing a consequential effect, an enforcement point outside the acting model shall validate principal, delegation, action, resource, environment, recipient, purpose, time, limits and required approval.
- **CP.12.R02** Bind approval to canonical action parameters and current policy/state versions; a changed material condition requires a new decision.
- **CP.12.R03** Refuse missing, expired, conflicting or unverified mandatory conditions and protect the gate and credentials from the governed agent.
- **CP.12.R04** Define atomic check/commit or destination-enforced version checks, replay resistance and the behavior for an unavailable authority service.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Authority at commitment. Compare EFA, FlowSignal and AI SAFE2 using changed state, changed target, and an independently observed effect.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
