# Comparative Analysis: AI SAFE² vs. Global Standards
**ID:** RN-2025-008 | **Focus:** Interoperability | **Status:** Verified

## 📉 The Problem: Fragmentation
Security leaders are forced to juggle disconnected frameworks:
*   **NIST AI RMF:** High-level risk management (Strategic).
*   **ISO 42001:** Management system requirements (Process).
*   **MITRE ATLAS:** Tactical attack vectors (Technical).

## 🚀 The AI SAFE² Advantage: Unified Execution
AI SAFE² v2.1 is built as the **Operational Bridge** that satisfies multiple standards simultaneously through single controls.

| Requirement Domain | NIST AI RMF (Map/Manage) | ISO/IEC 42001 (Annex A) | MITRE ATLAS | **AI SAFE² Control** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Integrity** | MAP 2.1 | A.8.2 Data Management | AML.T0043 | **[P1.T1.5_ADV]** Memory Hashing |
| **System Security** | MAN 1.3 | A.8.4 AI System Attack | AML.T0051 | **[P1.T1.2]** Prompt Firewall |
| **Supply Chain** | MAN 3.1 | A.8.2.4 Supplier Mgmt | AML.T0002 | **[P1.T1.2_ADV]** Model Signing |
| **Logging** | GOV 2.1 | A.8.3 Operations | AML.T0000 | **[P2.T3.1]** Immutable CoT Logs |
| **Human Oversight** | MAN 2.2 | B.9.2 Human Oversight | N/A | **[P4.T7.1]** HITL Workflows |

## 🎯 Conclusion
Implementing **AI SAFE² v2.1** does not replace NIST or ISO; it **operationalizes** them.
*   NIST tells you *what* to manage.
*   AI SAFE² gives you the *code and checklists* to manage it.

This "Rosetta Stone" approach reduces compliance overhead by approx. **40%** (Internal CSI benchmarking, 2025).
