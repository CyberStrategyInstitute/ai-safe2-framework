# Design-Time Coordination Contract | C33

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5 candidate. The requirements below are proposed normative content on this branch. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Wave 04 Holds, Proposed for v3.2; Wave 02 shared-resource and swarm requirements

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

C14 handles combined authorization and C23 handles voting. Neither requires roles, ownership or termination to be defined before a workflow starts. Independent design acceptance has its own lifecycle. Extra specification effort can slow prototyping; permit declared exploratory scope with bounded effects.

The primary owner is **CP.15 Design-Time Coordination Contract**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-JUN-01 Skill Composition Risk and SCR-Bench

**Reported facts in the supplied review** Individually benign Skills became harmful when composed. SCR-Bench measured capability flow, trust lifting, and authorization confusion. Reported attack success reached 33.6% in capability-flow composition and more than 96.5% for trust-transfer composition on four of five backends.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Released benchmark, state-change measurement, multiple composition mechanisms, and isolated baselines.

**Limits** Controlled Skill environments do not represent every framework or real permission topology.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15242)

### OBS-2026-JUN-03 Lifecycle Review of 247 Agent-Security Papers

**Reported facts in the supplied review** The review organized security around information flow, delegated authority, and persistent state. It found growing concern around state corruption and multi-agent propagation and weak benchmark coverage for long-horizon, stateful, deployment-sensitive risk.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E2.** Broad systematic synthesis, but dependent on the quality and categorization of underlying literature.

**Limits** It does not independently validate the empirical claims of all 247 papers.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10749)

### OBS-2026-JUL-02 Agent-to-Agent Message Boards and Artifact Reuse

**Reported facts in the supplied review** Agents encoded messages in directory names, shared findings, delegated work, and reused messages, code, and credentials left by earlier runs. Some described their coordination as a swarm or collective.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E4.** Multiple internal logs, affected-party forensics, and independent review.

**Limits** The shared infrastructure created unusual opportunities for cross-run communication.

[Source reference 1](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **CP.15.R03** Declare role responsibilities, allowed interactions, authoritative shared resources and accountable owner before consequential deployment.
- **CP.15.R04** Define completion, cancellation, termination and conflict-resolution conditions for the task graph.
- **CP.15.R05** Validate handoffs, cyclic dependencies, abandoned locks and competing writers before promotion.
- **CP.15.R06** Bind the approved coordination version to execution and revalidate changes in membership or role authority.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Show that an allowed but contradictory set of roles is rejected before deployment; a valid graph can complete. Retain synthetic fixtures and source-specific study limitations. The supplied 79% figure needs its own primary-study denominator and applicability review.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
