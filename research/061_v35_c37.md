# Declared Success Criteria Gate | C37

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5 candidate. The requirements below are proposed normative content on this branch. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Wave 04 Holds, Declared Success Criteria Gate; Wave 02 G-01 and P5 requirements

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

Evaluation cadence cannot compensate for a missing task objective. This gate applies at design and acceptance time, while C36 applies after execution. Exploratory and qualitative work need explicit human review criteria instead of fabricated machine precision.

The primary owner is **E5.5 Declared Success Criteria Gate**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-APR-01 Symbolic Guardrails for Domain-Specific Agents

**Reported facts in the supplied review** A review of 80 agent-security and safety benchmarks found that 85% did not state verifiable requirements. The authors found 74% of evaluated domain requirements could be enforced symbolically, with most requiring simple checks. Released artifacts support reproduction.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Broad benchmark review, enforceability analysis, empirical evaluation, and public artifacts.

**Limits** Symbolic rules work only where requirements can be represented precisely. They do not solve ambiguous intent, incomplete policy, or unknown future actions.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15579)

### OBS-2026-MAY-01 AgentSecBench and Intent-to-Execution Noninterference

**Reported facts in the supplied review** AgentSecBench formalized instruction integrity, retrieval confidentiality, and capability integrity under a common noninterference model. It distinguishes prompt annotations from enforcing projections that close an unauthorized information or capability channel before generation.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Formal games, paired adversarial and benign executions, and evaluation across six defense classes.

**Limits** Small Qwen models and exact-marker tests provide unambiguous ground truth but do not capture the full semantic problem.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26269)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **E5.5.R01** Declare intended benefit, observable success, prohibited effects and accepted uncertainty before consequential operation.
- **E5.5.R02** Use executable assertions where feasible and identify the responsible human rubric when judgment remains necessary.
- **E5.5.R03** Bind criteria to the workflow version and prevent the acting agent from relaxing them after observing its result.
- **E5.5.R04** Keep task completion, safety, utility and cost as separate outcomes.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Demonstrate that a workflow with no criteria cannot claim validated completion. Test correct assistance, safe refusal, false blocking and an approved exploratory task. Recheck the source of the supplied 41% figure.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
