# Data Source Mapping | C53

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5. The requirements below define the v3.5 framework. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Repository issue #390, provider-neutral source-rights observation proposal. The named AcqPath service is attributed context, not a required provider or independently verified integration.

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

Data Source Mapping already owns external-source identity and inventory. Add purpose, declaration, freshness and correction detail here, with CP.14 policy ownership. A new top-level rights control would duplicate source inventory and could imply legal authority. This placement accepts provider-neutral observations while preserving the separate permission decision. More metadata increases collection burden; minimize it and record unknowns.

The primary owner is **P2.T4.4 Data Source Mapping**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Issue #390 supplies a framework proposal. No named service execution, signature verification, legal applicability or production result is inferred.

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **P2.T4.4.R1** Record the externally sourced resource identifier, intended use, declaration state, observation time and freshness, provenance and integrity evidence before downstream policy evaluation.
- **P2.T4.4.R2** Keep the observation provider-neutral and distinguish observed declarations from licences, ownership, actual use and permission. UNKNOWN, stale or contradictory declarations cannot become permission by default.
- **P2.T4.4.R3** Bind reuse to the purpose and policy decision; record review/correction paths, minimization and unresolved rights or provenance conflicts without bypassing source access controls.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Test missing purpose, UNKNOWN, stale and conflicting declarations; preserve them as unresolved inputs and refuse a policy decision that treats them as permission. Verify the observation does not grant a licence, establish ownership, prove use or bypass access controls. Test a corrected declaration and a permitted purpose with separately recorded policy authority.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
