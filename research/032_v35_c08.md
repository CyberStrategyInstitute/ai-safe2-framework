# Agentic Control Plane Governance | C08

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5. The requirements below define the v3.5 framework. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Evidence waves 01-06; C08 disposition in docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md and docs/FRAMEWORK-UPDATE-ISSUES.md

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

Trusting content and granting authority are separate decisions within existing policy and isolation boundaries. One parent avoids divergent grants across surfaces.

The primary owner is **CP.4 Agentic Control Plane Governance**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-JUN-01 Skill Composition Risk and SCR-Bench

**Reported facts in the supplied review** Individually benign Skills became harmful when composed. SCR-Bench measured capability flow, trust lifting, and authorization confusion. Reported attack success reached 33.6% in capability-flow composition and more than 96.5% for trust-transfer composition on four of five backends.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Released benchmark, state-change measurement, multiple composition mechanisms, and isolated baselines.

**Limits** Controlled Skill environments do not represent every framework or real permission topology.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15242)

### OBS-2026-SEP-01 MMPIBench Multimodal Prompt Injection

**Reported facts in the supplied review** MMPIBench tested six visual carriers across six frameworks, five models, four attacker objectives, and 720 runs. Roughly 12.8% of attacks were attempted and about 1% completed. Audio exposure was narrower but much less defended, with attacks completing in 49% of evaluated cells and 75% for one model.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E3.** Reproducible benchmark, multiple modalities, frameworks, models, and execution-stage measurements.

**Limits** Audio coverage was sparse. Results depended heavily on model and framework support.

[Source reference 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09404)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **CP.4.R01** Separate information content from policy and action authority; untrusted content shall not add, widen or remove grants.
- **CP.4.R02** Treat source signatures as provenance and independently decide suitability, authorization and corroboration.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Information, provenance and authority. Verify that untrusted data can inform reasoning without changing grants and that shared-source claims cannot establish independence.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
