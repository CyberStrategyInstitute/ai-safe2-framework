# Behavioral Drift Baseline & Rollback | C17

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5 candidate. The requirements below are proposed normative content on this branch. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

Evidence waves 01-06; C17 disposition in docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md and docs/FRAMEWORK-UPDATE-ISSUES.md

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

Recovery owns fallback and reconstruction. Pin actual routing, preserve policy, and declare degraded capabilities. Wider fallback improves resilience but enlarges the trusted estate.

The primary owner is **F3.4 Behavioral Drift Baseline & Rollback**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

## Supporting reported findings

Supporting context for the failure mechanism; this does not establish that the source validates this proposed control or its applicability to every deployment.

### OBS-2026-AUG-03 Model Development Pacing and Universal Monitoring

**Reported facts in the supplied review** OpenAI reported universal monitoring for risky actions across agentic uses of an advanced model, stronger isolation, reduced shared services and standing privileges, continuous security testing, expanded chain-of-thought monitoring, and a temporary slowdown of a major training run.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E2.** First-party operational changes following an incident, but implementation details and effectiveness were not independently measurable.

**Limits** Internal controls and monitoring performance were not fully disclosed.

[Source reference 1](https://openai.com/hugging-face-incident-and-misalignment/)

### OBS-2026-MAY-06 Enterprise Agent Adoption and Shadow-Agent Survey

**Reported facts in the supplied review** The survey reported decentralized enterprise agent use, shadow agents, weak inventories, inconsistent runtime authorization, and slow detection. Nearly half of surveyed organizations reported an agent-related security incident.

**Source-assigned evidence** **E2-.** Useful adoption signal from an industry survey, but vendor sponsorship and respondent methodology constrain inference.

**Limits** Self-reported incidents were not independently validated or consistently defined.

[Source reference 1](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/enterprise-ai-security-starts-with-ai-agents)

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **F3.4.R01** Record actual model/provider/route identities and verify that fallback preserves applicable policy and authority.
- **F3.4.R02** Reassess material substitutions and exercise approved degraded operation without uncontrolled endpoint replacement.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Model retirement, routing drift and degraded operation. Verify outage handling and version-change requalification.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
