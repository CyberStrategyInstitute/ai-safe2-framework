How to validate a relationship

  1. Pin the publisher, edition and exact external clause. Check rights and applicability.
  2. Read the AI SAFE2 control and child clauses. Record supports, partial, addresses, contradicts or not-applicable with rationale.
  3. Identify actor, role, deployment scope, exclusions and the actual enforcement boundary.
  4. Attach appropriate independent evidence, reviewer, source revision, review date, expiry and revisit trigger.
  5. Run safe2 framework audit. A structural pass does not establish source meaning or legal compliance.
